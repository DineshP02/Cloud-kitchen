Where it started

A kitchen born out of one simple frustration.

We started Bowl & Hearth with a simple observation: local families and teams wanted food that was more thoughtful than a quick takeaway, without the planning required for a full restaurant meal.

Our cloud kitchen model lets us focus entirely on cooking, packing and delivery — no dining-room overhead. That means seasonal menus, focused prep and neighbourhood delivery, done right.

"Good food should fit into your day, not take over your day."
2019

One neighbourhood

The first delivery route served a tight local radius — twelve streets, zero fanfare, all heart.

2023

Office catering

Bulk meal trays for teams became a core part of our kitchen — offices wanted something better than sandwiches.

2026

12 delivery zones

A wider local network — still the same small-batch kitchen philosophy, just reaching more tables.

What drives us

Our promise

Four things you can count on, every single order.

01

Nothing pre-cooked, nothing frozen

Every dish starts from raw ingredients on the day it's ordered. We don't batch-cook a week ahead and reheat — that's not what "fresh" means to us.

02

A menu that follows the season

What's on offer shifts as local produce shifts. If it isn't in season nearby, it isn't on the menu — no exceptions for convenience.

03

Prices that don't move with delivery apps

What you see on our menu is what you pay. We keep pricing steady instead of surging it around mealtimes or weather.

04

A real person if something's wrong

No chatbot loops. If an order arrives late, cold, or wrong, you'll reach someone from our kitchen directly.
