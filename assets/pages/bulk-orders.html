Questions

Before you book.

Common questions from offices and event organisers placing bulk orders with us.

What's the minimum headcount for a bulk order? +

Working Lunch and Snack Table packages start from 15 people; Dinner Feast from 25 people. Smaller custom orders can be discussed directly.

How much advance notice do you need? +

24 hours for standard packages within city core; 48–72 hours for large events or suburb deliveries.

Can we customise the menu? +

Yes — mains, sides and dietary substitutions can be adjusted when you send your enquiry.

Do you provide serving staff or setup? +

Dinner Feast orders include basic setup at the venue on request; serving staff can be arranged for an additional fee.
